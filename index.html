How to use Tarini

  1. Ask. Hold the yellow buddy (or the Space bar) and talk, or type in the box. Tarini works out what kind of help you need.
  2. Show it something with +. Share your screen, use the camera, or add a photo, PDF or file. Tarini marks the exact spots on the picture.
  3. Learn on the whiteboard. Tap the board button next to the box, or ask "teach me" or "show me on the board". Tarini draws each step and explains it out loud.
  4. Children get hints, not answers. Tap the name at the top to switch person. The answer, settings and history need the parent PIN.

Floating buddy: the window icon at the top keeps a small Tarini on top of other programs.

Menu (top right): history of every conversation, the weekly report, light or dark mode and settings.

Tip: you can ask for fresh facts, for example "what is the price of copper today". Tarini searches the web and shows its sources.
