How to use Tarini

  1. Pick who is using it. Tap the name at the top. Children get hints, not answers.
  2. Pick a mode. The row of buttons under the header, such as Homework hints or Show me how.
  3. Show Tarini something. Share screen for anything on the computer, Camera for a notebook, or Add photo.
  4. Hold the yellow buddy and talk. Let go to send. A quick tap keeps listening until you tap again. You can also hold the Space bar or type.
  5. Follow the answer. Tarini speaks, writes the steps, and puts numbered circles on the picture.
  6. Keep going. Tap a suggested question, press Bigger hint, or press New topic to start fresh.

Working in another app? Press the window icon at the top for the floating buddy. It stays on top so you can ask without switching back.

Everything is saved by day in History, and the chart icon makes a weekly PDF report.
